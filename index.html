About the Club

Welcome to
Altona North SC

Founded in 1975 and based at D.N. Duane Reserve in Brooklyn, Altona North Soccer Club is a family-oriented club proudly representing Melbourne's western suburbs in a variety of Senior and Junior Football Victoria league competitions. We are a welcoming, inclusive club that caters to players of all ages.

From our senior men's squad to our junior elite teams, we offer a structured pathway for every player to grow, compete, and belong. ANSC is built on community, camaraderie, and a shared love of the game that has brought people together at Duane Reserve for over 50 years.

Competing in Football Victoria State League 2 North/West. Training and match days at Duane Reserve for dedicated senior players.

50+ Years Community | Culture | Passion

Founded in 1975, Altona North Soccer Club was built by the local community in Brooklyn - hard working families and footballers who wanted a club to call their own in Melbourne's western suburbs.

From humble beginnings on the fields of Duane Reserve, the club has grown into a proud competitor in Football Victoria's league structure. Our journey through the ranks - from State League 4 champions in 2020 to State League 3 champions in 2025 - reflects the dedication and passion of everyone who has pulled on the navy blue jersey. The inception of a Junior structure in 2019 stands strong and continues to grow year on year developing our future navy warriors.
